title: The Woolly app
description: See what your AI tools know, connect apps, and edit your memories.
---

```sh
woolly ui
```

This opens Woolly on your own computer at `http://127.0.0.1:11435`. Nothing on it is loaded from the internet. The [Mac app](https://github.com/rjcuff/woolly/releases/latest/download/Woolly-mac.dmg) has the same screens, starts Woolly in the background and lives in your menu bar.

## Home

How many memories you have, how many your apps saved each day, which apps are connected, and anything that needs review. Below that, the latest memories and which apps used Woolly most this week.

## Memories

Add a memory, search, and filter by project. Click a memory to edit it, and forget it with the trash button. Each memory shows the app that saved it.

## Apps

Every app Woolly supports, with Connect and Disconnect buttons. It does the same thing as `woolly connect`.

## Settings

Ollama status, sync between your devices, and where your memories are stored. From here you can open:

- **Conflicts:** something an app saved disagrees with what you said. Keep yours or use the new one.
- **History:** older versions of facts that changed. Make one current again.
- **Trash:** forgotten memories. Restore them, or empty the trash.
- **Activity:** what each app saved, read and forgot.

The page needs a secret token, and `woolly ui` passes it along for you. If you open the address directly, it asks you to use `woolly ui` instead.
