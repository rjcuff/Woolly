Every AI app you use forgets you, or keeps its own separate memory. Claude doesn't know what you told ChatGPT, Cursor doesn't know what you decided in Claude Code, and your teammates' agents start from zero.

**Woolly fixes that.** It runs on your laptop and gives all of your AI tools one shared memory, and with `woolly init`, one project memory for your whole team.

## Private by design

Woolly never sends your memories anywhere. There is no cloud, no telemetry and no sign-in. Everything stays in `~/.woolly` on your machine.

<Callout title="Do I need Ollama?">
  No. Woolly works fully on its own. If you already run [Ollama](/docs/ollama), Woolly also uses it to search by meaning, learn from your chats and save decisions when a session ends.
</Callout>
