Every AI app you use forgets you, or keeps its own separate memory. Claude doesn't know what you told ChatGPT, and Cursor doesn't know either.

**Woolly fixes that.** It runs on your laptop and gives all of your AI tools one shared memory.

## Why Woolly

<Cards>
  <Card title="One binary, no setup" description="No Docker, no database server, no account. Install it and it works." />
  <Card title="Files you can read" description="Every memory is a Markdown file. Edit it, search it, back it up, put it in git." />
  <Card title="Facts stay current" description="When something changes, the new fact replaces the old one automatically." />
  <Card title="You have the final say" description="If an AI contradicts something you said, Woolly flags it instead of overwriting you." />
</Cards>

## Private by design

Woolly never sends your memories anywhere. There is no cloud, no telemetry and no sign-in. Everything stays in `~/.woolly` on your machine.

<Callout title="Do I need Ollama?">
  No. Woolly works fully on its own. If you already run [Ollama](/docs/ollama), Woolly can use it for smarter search.
</Callout>
