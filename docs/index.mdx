Every AI app you use forgets you, or keeps its own separate memory. Claude doesn't know what you told ChatGPT, and Cursor doesn't know either.

**Woolly fixes that.** It runs on your laptop and gives all of your AI tools one shared memory.

## Why Woolly

## Private by design

Woolly never sends your memories anywhere. There is no cloud, no telemetry and no sign-in. Everything stays in `~/.woolly` on your machine.

<Callout title="Do I need Ollama?">
  No. Woolly works fully on its own. If you already run [Ollama](/docs/ollama), Woolly can use it for smarter search.
</Callout>
